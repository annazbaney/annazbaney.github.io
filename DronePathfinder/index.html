Mission log

    How it works

    • The map starts as an unmaintained field: rocks and weeds only. Transplants go in the soil only after the field is clear of rocks and weeds, in visible field rows.
    • Field pathway covers rock pickup, weed treatment, irrigation, pests & disease, and prepare field. Prepare field clears weeds and planted crop, then lays soil rows for planting. Treating weeds and irrigating both raise the stand. Each irrigate pass also makes crop plants grow larger, then sprouts more weeds, pests, and disease — treat them from the same pathway. If pests and disease are not treated, stand count falls after two irrigations.
    • Pollination is sequential: week 1 flowers, week 2 pollinates, week 3 sets fruit. Count pollinated plants reports how many of the stand are pollinated (week 2 or later). Fruit count totals fruit and leaves it on the plants. Harvest removes fruit.
    • After pesticide, treated weeds are removed so only standing crop remains.
    • The farm clock grows plants with the seasons — small in spring, leafy in summer, fruiting in fall, dormant in winter.
